Cardbox

Flashcards that stay on your phone.

Cardbox is a flashcard app that works entirely on your device. There is no account, no sign-up and no sync — what you make stays where you made it.

What it does

  • Write cards with a front, a back and an optional hint, or fill-in-the-blank sentences
  • Study in the order that suits you — shuffled, hardest first, weakest first, or proper spaced repetition (Leitner and SM-2)
  • Rename, add or remove the grading buttons; a single button is a valid setup
  • Switch a card off to park it without deleting it or losing its progress
  • Reminders at a fixed time, or a random one that picks a different line at a random hour
  • Colour palettes, fonts, corner roundness, text size, high contrast and reduced motion
  • Share a whole deck as a short code in any message, or export it as a file

Privacy

Cardbox collects nothing, shows no ads and needs no internet connection to work. Read the full privacy policy.
