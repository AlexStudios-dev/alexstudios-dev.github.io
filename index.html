Cardbox

Flashcards you shape to fit how you study.

Most flashcard apps have already decided things for you: the order the cards come in, what the grading buttons say, what the whole thing looks like. Cardbox hands those decisions back.

What you can change

  • Order a deck eleven ways — shuffle it, drill your worst cards first, or hand it to real spaced repetition and never think about scheduling again
  • The grading buttons themselves — rename, add or remove them, give each one its colour, and decide what each one does to a card’s schedule. One button is a valid setup.
  • Per-deck settings, so a grammar deck can behave nothing like a vocabulary deck
  • Colour palettes, light and dark, two visual styles, font, corner roundness, text size, high contrast and reduced motion
  • Which progress figures you see, and which you hide
  • Reminders at a fixed time, or a random one that picks a different line at a random hour

What it does not do

No account, no sign-up, no cloud, no sync, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Cardbox works offline from the first launch. Your decks stay on your phone. Read the full privacy policy.
