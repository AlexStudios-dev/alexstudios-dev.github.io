Privacy Policy — Cardbox

Last updated: 10 August 2026

Cardbox does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There is no account and no sign-up. Everything you create stays on your device.

Cardbox (“the app”) is developed by AlexStudios (“we”, “us”).

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking. We operate no servers and receive no information about you or about how you use the app.

What the app stores on your device

  • The decks and cards you create, including any text and images you add
  • Your study progress and statistics
  • Your reminders
  • Your settings, including themes and display preferences

All of this is held in the app’s own private storage on your device. None of it is uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes it.

An image you add to a card is copied into the app’s private storage so the card can keep displaying it. Your original photo is left untouched.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only to show the reminders you create. They are scheduled locally by your device; nothing is sent over the internet. You can decline this permission and the rest of the app still works.
  • Photos and files — requested only at the moment you choose to add a picture to a card or import a deck file, and only through your device’s own picker. The app receives only the single file you select.

Sharing

The app can copy a deck to your clipboard, or hand a file to your device’s share sheet, but only when you ask it to. What happens to it afterwards is between you and whichever app you send it to.

Links to other services

Choosing “Rate Cardbox” opens the Google Play Store, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

Cardbox collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
